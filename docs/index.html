M5Stack CoreS3 FTMS Remote

Dedicated Bluetooth Remote for Merach T-25B & FTMS Walking Pads

⚡ Quick Installation

Flash the firmware directly to your device via your web browser without installing PlatformIO or Python (requires Chrome or Edge):

  1. Connect your M5Stack CoreS3 SE to your computer using a USB-C data cable.
  2. If flashing fails to connect automatically, put the board into Download Mode: press and hold the red side button for 2–3 seconds until the internal green LED illuminates.
  3. Click Install below, choose the USB Serial port (typically usbmodem...), and confirm installation.

✨ Features

  • Dual Protocol: Reverse-engineered Merach vendor protocol (0xFFF2) for simultaneous speed & incline control plus FTMS (0x2ACD) telemetry.
  • Closed-Loop Control: Auto-detects motor ramping, mechanical stalls, and external remote adjustments.
  • Large Touch Steppers: Press-and-hold speed (MPH) and incline (%) cards designed for effortless use while walking.
  • Audible Speaker Feedback: Distinct confirmation, tick, and status tones.
